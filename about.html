About the Academy

About Silm Academy

Silm Academy is an Islamic educational institute in Vamanjoor, Mangalore. The academy’s stated focus is to make authentic Islamic knowledge accessible, structured and rooted in the classical tradition.

The academy’s learning approach includes Qur’anic memorization, Arabic language and Islamic sciences under the guidance described on the main website.

Learning at Silm Academy

Silm Academy aims to combine sound religious learning with character, sincerity and a lasting connection to faith. The main website provides information about programs, academy guidance, announcements and admissions.
