About The Academy

A Tradition of Sacred Learning

Silm Academy was founded with a single purpose: to make authentic Islamic knowledge accessible, structured, and rooted in the classical tradition. Our curriculum blends Qur'anic memorization, Arabic language, and the Islamic sciences under the guidance of qualified scholars.

We believe education is not only the transfer of information, but the cultivation of character, sincerity, and a lasting connection to one's faith.

Wellness Services

Certified Hypnotic Staff

Our certified team also offers therapeutic hypnotherapy and relaxation-based wellness sessions, alongside the academy's core Islamic education.

Hypnotism

Hypnotism utilizes deep relaxation and focused concentration to help individuals access their subconscious, fostering positive behavioral changes. It is commonly applied to manage stress, overcome phobias, break bad habits, and improve overall mental well-being.

Progressive Muscle Relaxation

Progressive Muscle Relaxation (PMR) is a proven technique that helps you reduce physical tension and relieve stress by intentionally tensing and then releasing specific muscle groups. This creates a deep mind-body connection, teaching you how to recognize and instantly melt away daily anxiety.
